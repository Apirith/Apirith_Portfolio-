Stormbolt Escape

A 10-player chase-runner where speed and strategy matter. Players race through three themed obstacle courses while a monster hunts them down. Reaching the finish line earns a leaderboard spot; getting caught sends you to spectate. Every run is shaped by level design, physics interactions, and the real threat of a creature that adapts to how you play.

Three arenas

  • Training Grounds (Easy) — Grass, bounce pads, laser gates
  • The Factory (Normal) — Industrial pistons, phasing floors, anti-gravity
  • Storm Peak (Hard) — Basalt cliffs, crumbling tiles, swinging hazards

Two monsters

  • The Speedster — Lunges at three times normal speed with glowing light trails
  • The Phantom — Drifts and blinks behind victims with a hooded wraith design

Under the hood: Server-side anti-cheat, 3D-licensed audio, autosave per server, up to 4 concurrent runs per level, and analytics tracking escape patterns and common failure points.

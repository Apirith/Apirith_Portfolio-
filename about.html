Building things with a mix of precision and curiosity.

My story

My work is rooted in making things tangible and useful. I like objects that sit at the intersection of engineering and personality: a product that feels considered, a system that reduces friction, and a sound that feels lived-in rather than generic.

That range shows up in my portfolio as CAD studies, product ideas, and audio experiments. Each one reinforces the same habit: practical exploration paired with intentional design.
